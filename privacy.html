This notice covers the .self launch and early access list.

Who is responsible?

findingtheself is responsible for your information. You can contact us at hello@bigredbox.co.uk.

What do we collect?

Your email address, optional first name and sign-up timestamp. We also keep the version of the consent text you agreed to. We do not store your raw IP address in our sign-up files.

For abuse prevention, we keep a salted hash derived from your IP address with request timestamps. Only records from the last day are used for rate limiting. Expired records are removed when the next sign-up request is processed; they may remain on disk until then. This is separate from the sign-up list. Hashes are pseudonymous data, not anonymous data.

Why, and on what basis?

To tell you about the .self launch and early access. We rely on your consent. You can use the website without signing up. The consent checkbox is never pre-ticked. There is no automated decision-making in this sign-up path.

Where is it stored? Who can read it?

The list is stored outside the website’s public directory, with restricted file permissions. A notification is sent through authenticated SMTP to an approved mailbox. Access to the list and mailbox is restricted to people authorised by findingtheself.

Hostinger provides hosting and mail delivery. Its services may process technical data and access logs separately from our sign-up files. Its data processing addendum describes its processing, subprocessors and transfer safeguards. Our no-cookies statement does not mean that the hosting provider keeps no logs.

How long do we keep it?

We keep your name and email only to tell you about the .self launch and early access. We delete the list within 30 days after the launch emails finish, or sooner if you ask.

How do I unsubscribe?

You can withdraw consent at any time. Email hello@bigredbox.co.uk any time and we'll remove you within 30 days. Every email will say how to unsubscribe.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your information, and withdraw your consent. You may also have rights to restrict processing and data portability where applicable. Use the contact above for a request.

You can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO).

Cookies and analytics

No cookies. No local storage. No analytics. No third-party scripts. The sign-up form works without JavaScript.
