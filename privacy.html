This notice describes the proposed .Self launch and early access list. Sign-up is switched off. No details are collected while sign-up is closed.

This notice is for legal review, not legal advice.

Who is responsible?

The .Self founders are the proposed controller. [PLACEHOLDER] Confirm the legal entity and controller contact address.

What would we collect?

Your email address, optional first name and sign-up timestamp. We would also keep the version of the consent text you agreed to. We do not store your raw IP address in our sign-up files.

For abuse prevention, the proposed script keeps a salted hash derived from your IP address with request timestamps. Only records from the last day are used for rate limiting. This is separate from the sign-up list. Hashes are pseudonymous data and are not described here as anonymous. [PLACEHOLDER] Confirm the cleanup schedule and maximum time expired rate records remain on disk.

[PLACEHOLDER] Confirm and disclose Hostinger’s own access logs, retention and processing before collecting details.

Why, and on what basis?

To tell you about the .Self launch and early access. The proposed lawful basis is your consent. The checkbox is optional to use the website and is never pre-ticked. There is no automated decision-making in this sign-up path.

Where would it be stored? Who could read it?

The proposed list would be stored outside the website’s public directory, with restricted file permissions. A notification would be sent to an approved owner mailbox.

You can withdraw consent at any time. [PLACEHOLDER] Set the contact address and approve the unsubscribe process before the first sign-up. Launch and early access emails must include a clear way to unsubscribe.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your information, and withdraw your consent. You may also have rights to restrict processing and data portability where applicable. [PLACEHOLDER] Confirm the contact address and process for rights requests.

No cookies. No local storage. No analytics. No third-party scripts. The proposed sign-up script uses no cookies and makes no external API calls. Host mail delivery is subject to approval.
