# .Self badge

The logo is lowercase `.self`, traced as outlined paths in the supplied straight master. The product name in sentences is `.Self`. Founders to confirm this distinction before launch.

- `badge.svg`: straight, 512 × 512 artwork with a plum rounded square (26-unit radius).
- `badge-slant.svg`: identical paths and colours, rotated 3 degrees clockwise. The expanded 540 × 540 viewBox leaves transparent space around the artwork and prevents clipping.
- `badge-512.png` and `badge-slant-512.png`: 512 × 512 exports, with transparency outside the badge shape.
- `favicon.svg`, `favicon.png` (32px) and `touch-icon.png` (180px): the same slanted artwork.
- Header: 44px. Footer: 36px. Tiles: 180px (140px on tablets, 160px on narrow screens). Minimum display size: 24px. Inspected at 24, 44, 180 and 512px.

Use the supplied outlines unchanged. Never retype the logo in a font, recolour or stretch it. White on plum (#FFFFFF on #60334F) only; transparent outside the rounded square. No filters, scripts, fonts or external references.

Allow clear space equal to one letter-height around the badge. The master’s tall letters occupy approximately 39% of the straight square’s height; for a 44px badge, allow about 17px around it. For a 180px slanted asset, allow about 66px around the visible artwork. Do not put other text or marks into that space.

Use the slanted version in the website header, footer and the two large tiles. Keep the straight master for small-size or other approved applications. In running sentences use the text name `.Self`, without an inline badge.
